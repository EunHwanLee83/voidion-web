App name: Nom Nom Mimic (미믹 키우기)

Users of "Nom Nom Mimic" can delete their account and game data at any time directly within the game. It is free of charge, and you do not need to give a reason.

How to delete

Method 1 — Delete immediately within the game (recommended)
  1. Launch the game.
  2. Open ⚙ Settings on the right side of the screen.
  3. Tap [🗑 Delete Account (Withdraw)] at the very bottom.
  4. Read the notice and confirm twice, and the account is deleted on the spot (account information remaining on the server is deleted as shown in the table below: within 30 days after the 3-month access-log retention period has passed). However, for users residing in Taiwan, game data is kept for 30 days and then erased, as required by Taiwan law (Terms of Service, Article 55, paragraph 8).

Being logged in is itself the identity verification, so there is no separate identity verification procedure or waiting period. When the deletion is finished, the app closes, and if you launch it again, it starts with a new account.

Method 2 — By email (if you cannot install the app)
  1. Send an email to info@voidion.kr.
  2. Write "Account deletion request" in the subject line.
  3. In the body, write your in-game nickname (for example, 용감한미믹#1234) or your account number. You can find your account number on the 👤 Account screen in the game.

Requests received by email are processed within 10 days of the date of receipt after your identity is verified, and we will notify you of completion at your reply address.

However, for users residing in Taiwan, the Company refunds it within 30 days even after account deletion, in accordance with Article 55, paragraph 16 of the Terms of Service.
