General Notes

  • All probabilities are expressed as percentages (%), and they apply to each individual summon independently of all other summons.
  • Probabilities are determined by drawing the grade first, then drawing an individual within that grade with equal probability — the values in the table are Grade probabilities, and the probability of a single individual is grade probability ÷ number of types in that grade.
  • In summons, a mechanism (pity/guarantee) that guarantees a higher grade after a set number of summons is not operated. For equipment, skills, and allies, the Summon Level is not a guaranteed grant; it is a mechanism by which the probability table itself improves — all of those values are listed in the per-level table for each item.
  • The probabilities in the tables are rounded, so their totals may differ slightly from 100% in the decimal places.
  • If the probabilities change, the changes and their effective date will be announced in advance.
