  • Account linking is an optional feature. All game content can be used without linking an account, and if you do not link an account, no email address is collected.
  • No password is created. Account linking and transfer verify your identity only by a 6-digit verification code sent by email each time, so the Company (VOIDION) neither receives nor stores passwords.
  • A verification code disappears automatically after 10 minutes. Once verification succeeds, the code expires immediately, and the same code cannot be used again.
  • A linked email address is kept only in the list used to find which account that address belongs to. No advertising or promotional emails are sent to it, and no emails other than verification codes are sent.
  • You do not need to enter an email address when sending an inquiry. If you do, it is used only to send the reply, and it is not kept separately after the inquiry has been handled.
  • Phone numbers, resident registration numbers, and location information are not collected.
  • Toss Login in the Apps in Toss version uses only the member identifier (userKey) needed to identify the account. The name that Toss also transmits is not stored, and email address, gender, date of birth, phone number, and Connecting Information (CI) are not requested.
  • The Company does not use any separate tool that collects app error or crash logs.

2. Processing and Retention Period of Personal Information

  • Personal information is retained for the period during which the service is provided, and is destroyed without delay when the user requests deletion or deletes the account.
  • A verification code expires automatically 10 minutes after it is issued or once verification succeeds.
  • A linked email address is removed from the list immediately when the account is deleted. The same applies when the user withdraws directly within the game.
  • Where retention is required by applicable laws (for example, payment and refund records for 5 years under the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (전자상거래 등에서의 소비자보호에 관한 법률)), the information is retained for that period.

Through contracts and terms with those processors, the Company maintains a system to continuously take measures equivalent to those required under the APPI, and will provide details upon request. Information on each country's personal information protection system is available from Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission (Republic of Korea / Guidelines on provision to third parties in foreign countries). The United States has no comprehensive federal privacy law; sector-specific and state laws apply.
  • Security control measures (APPI Art. 23) — A designated personal information protection officer (organizational) · handling rights limited to that officer (personnel) · storage in the secured facilities of cloud providers rather than an office (physical) · access control, encrypted communication (HTTPS), and no receipt or storage of passwords (technical) · handling with an understanding of the systems in Korea and the United States (external environment).
  • Requests for disclosure, etc. — You may request notification of purposes of use, disclosure, correction/addition/deletion, suspension of use or erasure, suspension of provision to third parties, and disclosure of third-party provision records by email to the contact in Section 8.
