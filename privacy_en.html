  • Long-unused accounts — For an account that has not been accessed for one year or more, personal information may be stored separately or destroyed after notice is given at least 30 days in advance, under Article 10 of the Terms of Service.

5. Procedures and Methods of Destroying Personal Information, and Security Measures

Destruction procedure

Personal information whose retention period has elapsed or whose purpose of processing has been achieved is destroyed without delay. Information that must be retained under applicable laws is stored separately from other personal information and is destroyed without delay when that period ends.

Destruction method

Information in the form of electronic files is deleted by a method that makes recovery or reproduction impossible. Personal information printed on paper is shredded or incinerated.

Security measures

  • Administrative measures — The personal information protection officer handles the information directly, and the number of people who can access personal information is limited to the minimum.
  • Technical measures — Restricting access rights to servers and management tools, encrypting transmission (HTTPS), neither receiving nor storing passwords, and storing only a converted value instead of the email address in verification records.
  • Physical measures — There is no in-house server room; information is stored in the secured facilities of cloud providers (such as Microsoft Azure).
